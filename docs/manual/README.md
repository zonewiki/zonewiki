# Zonewiki's manual

How to use Zonewiki: a wiki with a task manager and quick notes, for you, your team and your AI assistants.
Zonewiki is first-class for people and for assistants: it's made to be a pleasure to use in your browser, and your assistant can do everything you can, never more.
It's also in Zonewiki itself, under **Help**, and your assistant can look things up in it.

New to Zonewiki? Start with [Getting started](getting-started.md).
