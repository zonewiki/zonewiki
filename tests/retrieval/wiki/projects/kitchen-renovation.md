# Kitchen renovation

## Scope

The cabinets from 2011 stay. New: an oak worktop, an induction hob in place
of the gas one, LED strips under the wall units, and the wall behind the
sink tiled.

## Budget

| Item | Estimate |
|------|----------|
| Worktop, oak, 4.2 m | 1,150 € |
| Induction hob | 780 € |
| Electrician (new 400 V line) | 620 € |
| Tiles and tiler | 540 € |
| Lighting | 160 € |

Ceiling of 3,500 € agreed with Ana; anything above waits until autumn.

## Decisions

- Oak, oiled, not laminate: it can be sanded when it stains.
- The gas connection is capped by the utility, not removed.
- No island: the room is 2.9 m wide.

## Open questions

- Tiles up to the wall units or only 30 cm?
