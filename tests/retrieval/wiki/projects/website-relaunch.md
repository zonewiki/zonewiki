# Website relaunch

## Goals

1. People book a repair slot online instead of phoning.
2. The price list is one page, not a PDF.
3. The site loads fast on a phone in the yard's weak reception.

## Decisions

- Static site, built from Markdown, hosted with the mail provider.
- Booking through a form that writes into the shared calendar; no accounts.
- German first, English as a second language for the tourists in summer.
- Photos by Mira, no stock pictures.
