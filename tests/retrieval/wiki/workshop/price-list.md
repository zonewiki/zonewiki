# Price list

Labour is 68 € an hour, charged in quarter hours. Parts extra.

## Fixed prices

| Job | Price |
|-----|-------|
| Puncture, tube replaced | 14 € |
| Brake pads, per wheel | 19 € |
| Chain replaced | 22 € |
| Gears adjusted | 18 € |
| Wheel trued | 24 € |
| Small service | 59 € |
| Full service | 119 € |

## E-bikes

Software update and diagnosis 35 €. Motor work only on the two brands we
are trained for; others go to the dealer.

## Discounts

Students and apprentices 10 % on labour. Members of the cycling club 5 % on parts.
