# Service checklist

What a full service covers, in the order we do it.

## Frame and wheels

- Frame and fork checked for cracks, especially at the welds
- Headset and bottom bracket: play and noise
- Wheels trued, spokes tensioned, hubs adjusted

## Drivetrain

- Chain wear measured; replaced above 0.75 %
- Cassette and chainrings checked for shark teeth
- Gears indexed, cables oiled or replaced

## Brakes

- Pads: replaced below 1 mm
- Discs measured, replaced below 1.5 mm
- Hydraulic brakes bled every second year

## Before it leaves

Test ride around the yard, tyres to the pressure on the sidewall, lights
tested, the customer told what will be due next.
