# Opening hours

Radwerk is open Tuesday to Friday 9–12 and 14–18, Saturday 9–13.
Closed on Mondays and Sundays.

## Season

From April to September also Thursday until 20:00.
Between Christmas and Epiphany the workshop is closed.

## Express repairs

A flat tyre or a broken chain is fixed while you wait, if you come before 11.
Everything else by appointment.
