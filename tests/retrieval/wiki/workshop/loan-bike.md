# Loan bike

One city bike, three-speed, with a basket and a lock. For customers whose
repair takes longer than we said.

## Rules

Free for a day; from the second day 8 € a day. A copy of an ID and a phone
number go into the book. No loan to anyone under 16 without a parent.

## Care

Checked every Monday that isn't a holiday: tyres, lights, the lock's key
on its hook.
