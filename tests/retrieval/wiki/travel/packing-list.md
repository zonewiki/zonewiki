# Packing list

## Always

Passports or ID cards, the health insurance cards, chargers and the
travel adapter, a power bank, the first-aid pouch, sunglasses.

## City trip

Comfortable shoes that are already broken in. One warm layer even in
summer. A small umbrella. The folding bag for what gets bought.

## Hiking

Boots, two pairs of walking socks per day minus one, rain jacket and
trousers, hat and gloves above 2,000 m even in August, head torch, the
paper map, blister plasters.

## With the children

A change of clothes in the hand luggage. Snacks. The audiobooks downloaded
before leaving, not at the airport.
