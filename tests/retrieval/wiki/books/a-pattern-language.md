# A Pattern Language

Christopher Alexander, 1977. Notes on the patterns that matter for our house.

## Light on two sides

A room lit from one side has glare and deep shadows; people avoid it. The
kitchen has one window: the reason it feels dark at the table. A roof
light would be the second side.

## Sitting wall

A low wall, 40 cm, where a garden meets a path: a place to sit that also
marks the edge. Planned between the yard and the vegetable beds.

## Workspace enclosure

A desk works with a wall behind and to one side, and a view ahead of more
than two metres. The study's desk faces the wall: turn it.
