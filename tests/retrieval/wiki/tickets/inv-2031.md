# INV-2031: Brake pads ran out twice

Reported by Jonas on 24 February.

## What happened

Disc brake pads of the common type were out of stock on 11 and on 20
February. Both times the spreadsheet said eight pairs.

## Cause

Pads taken for warranty repairs were never entered: nobody writes down a
part that isn't billed.

## Fix

Warranty parts leave the bin like any other and move the card. The
spreadsheet is retired with the bins ([[Projects/Inventory system]]).

Status: closed on 6 March.
