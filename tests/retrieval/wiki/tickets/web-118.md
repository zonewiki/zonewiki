# WEB-118: Booking form sends no confirmation

Reported by Tom on 27 March.

## What happened

A test booking arrived in the calendar, but the customer got no mail.

## Cause

The form's sender address wasn't allowed to send for our domain; the mail
provider dropped it silently.

## Fix

The sender is now `termine@radwerk.example`, which is set up at the provider.
Tested with three addresses at different mail services.

Status: closed on 31 March.
