# INV-2040: Minimums for tubes are too low

Reported by Mira on 18 March.

## What happened

After a sunny weekend all 28-inch tubes were gone by Tuesday noon. The
minimum of ten is one good Saturday.

## Proposal

Seasonal minimums: thirty from April to September, ten in winter. The card
gets two numbers.

## Status

Open. Jonas checks last summer's invoices for the real numbers.
