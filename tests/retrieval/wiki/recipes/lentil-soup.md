# Lentil soup

The weeknight one. Forty minutes, most of it unattended.

## Ingredients

- 250 g red lentils
- 1 onion, 2 carrots, 2 cloves of garlic
- 1 tin of tomatoes
- 1 l vegetable stock
- Cumin, smoked paprika, a bay leaf
- Lemon juice and yoghurt to serve

## Method

Sweat onion, carrots and garlic in oil. Add the spices for a minute, then
lentils, tomatoes and stock. Simmer 25 minutes. Blend half of it, season
with lemon.

Freezes well: portions for two in the flat boxes.
