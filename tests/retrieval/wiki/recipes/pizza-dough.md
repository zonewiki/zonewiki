# Pizza dough

For four pizzas. Made the day before, it tastes better and stretches easier.

## Ingredients

| | Amount |
|---|---|
| Flour, tipo 00 | 600 g |
| Water | 390 g |
| Salt | 15 g |
| Fresh yeast | 2 g |

## Method

1. Dissolve the yeast in the water, add flour, then salt. Knead 10 minutes.
2. Two hours at room temperature, then 18 to 24 hours in the fridge.
3. Four balls, two hours to come back to room temperature.
4. Oven as hot as it goes, stone or steel preheated for 45 minutes. 6 to 7 minutes.

## Toppings we like

Tomato, mozzarella, basil. Or potato, rosemary and taleggio, without tomato.
