# When the internet is down

## First

Look at the router in the hallway cupboard. Power and DSL should be solid
green, Internet solid or blinking green.

## DSL blinks

The line is down. Wait ten minutes; if it stays, call the provider. The
customer number is on the label under the router.

## Everything green, still nothing

Unplug the router for thirty seconds. It takes about three minutes to come
back. If only the upstairs has no connection, it is the repeater: see [[House/Wifi]].

## Never

Don't press the reset pin at the back: it wipes the settings, and the access
data would have to be typed in again.
