# Fitting a bike to a rider

## Saddle height

Heel on the pedal at its lowest point: the leg is just straight. Then the
ball of the foot on the pedal gives the right bend in the knee.

## Saddle position

With the cranks level, a plumb line from the front of the knee falls
through the pedal axle. Move the saddle on its rails, not the post.

## Handlebar

For touring, level with the saddle or slightly above. Numb hands mean too
much weight on them: raise the bar or shorten the stem.

## Check after a week

People adjust. Offer a free check after the first hundred kilometres.
