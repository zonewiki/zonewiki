# Backing up the photo library

The photos live on the desktop in the study. Two copies exist besides:
the disk in the drawer and the one at Ana's parents'.

## Monthly

1. Plug in the disk labelled "Fotos A".
2. Start the backup program and choose "Photo library".
3. Wait for "Verified", about twenty minutes, then eject and unplug.

## Twice a year

Swap "Fotos A" with "Fotos B" at Ana's parents' when visiting, so one copy
is always out of the house.

## Restoring

Open the backup program, choose the date, and restore to a new folder first:
never over the library itself.
