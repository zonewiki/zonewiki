# Tomatoes

## Varieties

Ochsenherz for salads, San Marzano for sauce, a yellow cherry tomato for the
children to pick. Six plants are enough; ten were too many in 2025.

## Calendar

- Sow indoors mid March, on the south window.
- Prick out when the first true leaves show.
- Plant out after the Ice Saints, mid May, under the roof by the wall.
- Pinch out side shoots weekly from June.

## Problems

Blight came in August 2024 after two wet weeks. Since then: under the roof
only, water the soil and never the leaves, and remove the lowest leaves.
