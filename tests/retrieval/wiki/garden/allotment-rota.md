# Allotment water rota

The allotment's shared pump is run by one household each week, in the
order of the plots. Ours is plot 14.

## Our weeks in 2026

Weeks 19, 27, 35 and 43.

## What the duty is

Unlock the pump house at 7:00 and lock it at 20:00, check the oil glass,
and write the meter into the green book on Sunday evening.

## Swapping

Swap with plot 13 or 15 by writing it on the board at the gate, a week ahead.
