# Compost

Two bins behind the lilac: one being filled, one resting.

## What goes in

Kitchen scraps without meat or cooked food, coffee grounds with the filter,
grass in thin layers, leaves, shredded cardboard.

## What stays out

Citrus peel in quantity, diseased tomato plants, weeds with seeds, cat litter.

## Turning

The resting bin is turned in April and September. It is ready when it smells
of forest floor and nothing in it is recognisable, about nine months.
