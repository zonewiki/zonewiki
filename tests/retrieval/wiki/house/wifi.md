# Wifi

## Networks

`Lindenhof` for us, `Lindenhof-Gast` for visitors, which can't see the NAS
or the printer. The guest password is on the card on the fridge and changes
at New Year.

## Repeater

Upstairs in the corridor socket. When the upstairs is slow or offline, pull
it out, wait ten seconds, plug it in. Its light is white when it is linked
and orange when it has lost the router.

## Workshop

The workshop has its own access point on the cable through the yard. The
till and the card terminal are on the cable, never on wifi.
