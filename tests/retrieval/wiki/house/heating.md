# Heating

A gas condensing boiler from 2014 in the cellar, radiators upstairs,
underfloor heating on the ground floor.

## Settings

Day 21 °C from 6:00 to 22:00, night 17 °C. The underfloor circuit reacts
slowly: change it by one degree and wait a day.

## Refilling

The pressure should be between 1.2 and 1.8 bar when cold. Below 1 bar the
boiler shows F22 and stops: open the filling tap under the boiler until the
gauge reads 1.5, then close it firmly.

## Service

Every autumn by the installer; the chimney sweep comes by himself in March.
Error codes are on the sticker inside the boiler's flap.
