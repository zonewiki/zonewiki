## Washing machine

Error E18 means the drain pump is blocked: open the flap at the bottom
right, let the water into a tray, unscrew the filter and clean it.

## Dishwasher

Salt every six weeks; the water here is hard (21 °dH). Clean the sieve when
glasses come out cloudy.
