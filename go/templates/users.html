A user is put in a group in the list above; anyone who may write also makes groups of their own on their Groups page, and says who is in those (their owners). A group can also be made of other groups: whoever is in one of them is in it, so a task list for two teams is for one group, and who joins a team later is in it at once. Taking a group out takes what is for the larger group from its people.

Roles

admin: manages the users, and changes everything shared. editor: changes everything shared. viewer: reads what is shared, and changes nothing, not even private pages or notes of their own. No one, an admin neither, sees another user's private pages or knows their password. An admin can reset a user's password, though, and whoever opens that link signs in as them: the user notices, as their old password stops working. You can't disable or remove yourself here: another admin can.
